🛠️ Equipment & Shopping Guide

Where to Shop in Denver for Aquascaping Supplies

🐠 Local Fish Stores

🏪 The Fish Den

Address:
15940 E Colfax Ave, Aurora, CO 80011
Hours:
Mon-Sat 10am-7pm, Sun 10am-6pm
Phone:
(303) 366-4144
Best For:
Cherry shrimp, basic equipment, beginner-friendly staff

What We'll Find Here: Live plants (usually Anubias, Java Fern, basic stems), cherry shrimp, Neocaridina varieties, basic equipment (filters, heaters, lights), aqua soil, and helpful staff who know their stuff!

Ask about their plant shipment days! Plants are freshest Wed/Thu usually. Cherry shrimp selection is excellent.

🪨 Aquarocks

Address:
4115 S Broadway, Englewood, CO 80113
Hours:
Tue-Sat 11am-6pm, Sun 12-5pm, Closed Monday
Phone:
(303) 781-8278
Best For:
Hardscape materials, rocks, driftwood, unique aquascaping supplies

What We'll Find Here: TONS of hardscape options! Dragon stone, seiryu stone, lava rock, spider wood, manzanita. This is the place for building your aquascape layout. Also carries premium substrates and specialty plants.

Bring photos of scapes you like! Staff can help select compatible rocks/wood. They sell by the pound, so you can mix and match to create the perfect layout.

🎨 Thrift & Craft Stores

🎭 Michael's

Locations:
Multiple Denver locations - check michaels.com for nearest
Best For:
Unique tank shapes! Glass vases, bowls, and containers

Hidden Gems: Glass vases section has amazing options for planted jars and nano tanks! Look for wide-mouth cylinders, fish bowls, apothecary jars. Often 40-50% off with coupons.

Download the Michael's app for coupons BEFORE we go. Also check clearance section - sometimes find glass containers for $5-10 that make perfect aquascapes!

♻️ Thrift Stores (Arc, Goodwill, Savers)

Best For:
Unique tanks, glass containers, budget finds

Treasure Hunting: You never know what you'll find! Glass bowls, unique vases, vintage aquariums, interesting containers that make unconventional aquascapes. Always check for cracks!

  • Arc Thrift - Colorado locations - Great housewares section
  • Goodwill stores - Hit or miss but worth checking
  • Savers (Value Village) - Better curation, decent glass selection
Thrift stores restock Wed-Thu mornings. Visit early for best selection! Bring your phone to Google "is this aquarium safe" for any unusual finds.

🌐 Online Resources

When to Order Online

  • Rare plants: BucePlant, Aquarium Co-Op, Modern Aquarium - better selection than local
  • Specialty shrimp: Flip Aquatics, Shrimp Farm USA - higher grades, specific lines
  • Equipment deals: Amazon, BulkReefSupply - often cheaper than local for filters, CO2
  • Dry goods: Substrate, ferts, hardscape if buying in bulk

💰 Money-Saving Tips

Buy used tanks on Facebook Marketplace or Craigslist! People upgrade constantly. A $40 tank can be found for $15-20. Just check for leaks (fill with water outside for 24hrs).
Join "Colorado Aquarium Society" Facebook group. Members sell plants, shrimp, and equipment locally. Great deals and experienced hobbyists!
Start small and upgrade. A 5-gallon planted jar makes an amazing first project and costs under $100 total. Master it, then scale up!
