Welcome to Your Personal Aquascaping Workshop

This site is your guide to building a beautiful, thriving shrimp tank together!

Your Gift: A day where I teach you everything about aquascaping, and we build an amazing ecosystem for shrimp and snails. This is our project — we'll pick out plants, design the hardscape, and create something living and beautiful that you can enjoy every day.

Everything you need to know is here, and we'll explore it all together! 💚

Choose Your Tank Style

🌱

Walstad Method

Low-tech, natural ecosystem. Dirted substrate creates a self-balancing environment. Minimal maintenance, maximum nature.

 ♻️

Self-Sustaining Tank

The "set it and forget it" approach. Balanced ecosystem with hardy plants and simple care. Perfect for beginners!

 ✨

High-Tech Planted

CO2 injection, strong lighting, nutrient dosing. Stunning plant growth and vibrant colors. More involved but spectacular results!

What to Expect on Our Workshop Day

  • We'll visit The Fish Den or Aquarocks to pick out plants, shrimp, and hardscape materials
  • Maybe hunt for a unique tank shape at Michael's or local thrift stores
  • I'll teach you aquascaping principles: rule of thirds, creating depth, choosing focal points
  • Hands-on building: substrate layering, planting techniques, hardscape arrangement
  • Set up filtration, lighting, and get your tank cycling
  • Lunch together at a great Denver spot (my treat!)
  • You take home a thriving ecosystem you created — and the knowledge to maintain it!
